# Code of conduct

This project exists to be argued with. Disagreement about an item, a label or a metric is the point, and a
clinician who says an annotation is wrong is doing the most valuable work here.

What is expected:

- Argue about the item, the evidence and the method, not about the person.
- Say what you actually observed and what you inferred, and keep the two apart.
- Assume the other side read the protocol; if they did not, point at the section rather than at them.
- No personal data in issues, discussions or pull requests, and no patient information of any kind.

What is not tolerated: harassment, personal attacks, sustained disruption, or publishing anyone's private
information. Maintainers may edit, lock or remove contributions that breach this, and may block accounts.

To report something, email the maintainer listed in `CITATION.cff` or use GitHub's private reporting.
Reports are handled confidentially.
